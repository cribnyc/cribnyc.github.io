What is Crib?

Crib is a new apartment hunting platform that connects renters with trusted Viewers who visit apartments on their behalf. No more wasting time traveling to properties that don't meet your needs.

Whether you're relocating to a new city, have a busy schedule, or just want to save time, Crib makes finding your perfect apartment easier than ever.

How It Works

🏠

For Renters

Post your apartment requirements and budget. Browse Viewers in your area by their rates and reviews. Get detailed video tours and reports without leaving your home.

📹

For Viewers

Set your own rates and work on your schedule. Help renters find their perfect home while earning money. Build your reputation through reviews and grow your business.
