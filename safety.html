Safety Information

Your peace of mind is built into everything we do.

Our Commitment to Your Safety

At Crib, your safety and peace of mind are at the center of everything we build. Whether you're across the city or across the world, we want you to feel fully confident that the Viewer visiting an apartment on your behalf is vetted, reliable, and accountable.

How We Keep You Safe
1
Verified Viewers

Every Viewer on the Crib platform is required to complete our ~placeholder~ verification process before they can accept bookings.

  • Government-issued ID verification
  • Background checks (conducted where applicable under applicable law)
  • Review of past ratings and platform history
2
Secure In-App Messaging Only

To protect your privacy, all communication between Tour Requestors and Viewers takes place exclusively within Crib's secure in-app chat. Crib will never share your personal contact information with a Viewer unless you explicitly choose to do so.

Important: If a Viewer attempts to contact you outside the app, or asks for personal information, please report this immediately via in-app support.

3
Transparent Viewer Profiles

Before booking a tour, you can review your Viewer's full profile, which includes:

  • A personal bio
  • Mandatory Profile Picture
  • Ratings and reviews from real users
  • Number of completed viewings
4
Safety Guidelines for Users

To keep our community safe, all users are asked to observe the following guidelines:

  • Never send payment or share personal information outside the Crib app
  • Only book tours through official Crib channels
  • Report any suspicious behavior immediately via the app or by emailing safety@cribapp.com
  • Confirm the building address and access details with your Viewer before the tour begins
5
Viewer Role & Limitations

Crib Viewers are independent contractors who perform a physical access and visual relay service only. Viewers are not licensed real estate brokers or agents, and may not:

  • Negotiate lease or rental terms on your behalf
  • Sign any documents or agreements
  • Access private or restricted areas of a building without prior permission

A Viewer's role is to observe, record, and report back - nothing more. Please see our Terms of Service for full details.

If Something Doesn't Feel Right

We take every safety concern seriously. If you feel uncomfortable about a Viewer, a listing, or any interaction on the platform, please contact us right away.

Support: support@cribapp.com

We respond to urgent safety messages within 1 hour during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9AM–6PM ET).
