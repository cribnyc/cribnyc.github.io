Safety Information

Our Commitment to Your Safety

At Crib, your safety and trust are at the center of everything we build. Whether you're across the city or across the world, we ensure every Viewer on our platform is vetted, accountable, and transparent.

How We Keep You Safe

1

Verified Viewers

All Viewers must complete our verification process before accepting bookings.

  • Government-issued ID verification
  • Background checks where applicable
  • Review of ratings and activity history
2

Secure In-App Communication

All communication stays within Crib’s platform to protect your privacy.

Important: Never share personal information or move conversations outside the app.
3

Transparent Profiles

  • Viewer bios and photos
  • Ratings and reviews
  • Completed tours history
4

User Safety Guidelines

  • Only book and pay through Crib
  • Verify listing details beforehand
  • Report suspicious behavior immediately
5

Viewer Role & Limitations

Viewers are not real estate agents and cannot:

  • Negotiate leases
  • Sign documents
  • Access restricted areas

If Something Doesn’t Feel Right

If you feel uncomfortable at any point, please contact us immediately.

Email: support@teamcribnyc.com
